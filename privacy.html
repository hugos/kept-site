Privacy policy

Kept shows no ads, never sells or shares your data, and doesn’t track you across apps or websites. It keeps only what each feature needs to work — and this page says exactly what that is.

Who is responsible

Kept is made by Hugo Silva, a self-employed developer in Portugal, who is the data controller for the purposes of the EU’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: keptfinances@gmail.com.

What Kept never does

  • Show advertising, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Sell your data, or “share” it as the word is used in US privacy laws.
  • Track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Use your financial data to train artificial intelligence models.

Your figures and entries

Everything you enter or import — income, bills, spending, categories, notes — is stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud database, which belongs to your Apple Account. The developer has no access to it. Apple stores it as part of iCloud, under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Bank statements

When you import a statement, Kept reads the PDF on your iPhone. The file is never uploaded.

If Kept can’t read a statement, you can choose to send its layout to the developer. Before anything is sent, every letter is replaced with “a” and every number with “9”, keeping only column headings and month names, so no names, amounts or account numbers are included. You see the complete text first and send it yourself, by email.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple. Kept learns from Apple, on your device, only whether your subscription is active. It never sees your payment details.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in your iPhone’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple sends crash reports and basic performance data to the developer. They contain no figures or entries from Kept and are not linked to you. Kept has no analytics of its own.

Connecting a bank account

This feature is not available yet. This section describes how it will work when it launches, so that you know in advance. Using it will always be optional, and the rest of Kept works without it.

  • Read-only. Kept can see accounts, balances and transactions. It can never move money.
  • Through a licensed provider. Connections are made through Plaid Inc. You sign in to your bank on your bank’s or Plaid’s own screens; Kept never sees your bank password. Plaid’s handling of your data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.
  • An account with Kept. To keep a connection working, Kept needs to know whose it is, so you sign in with Apple. Apple can hide your email address from us.
  • What Kept’s servers keep: your Sign in with Apple identifier; your subscription status; for each connection, the bank’s name, an encrypted access key and where the last update stopped. Nothing else.
  • Your transactions are passed from the provider to your iPhone and stored there and in your iCloud, like everything else. Kept’s servers do not keep them.
  • Ending it. Disconnecting a bank, deleting your Kept account, or letting your subscription lapse removes the connection at Plaid and deletes what Kept’s servers held about it.

If you email us

We receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply. Messages are kept for as long as needed to help you, and deleted on request.

Why we may process your data

Providing the app you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)); your consent, for connecting a bank account (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time by disconnecting; and our legitimate interest in answering support requests and fixing faults (Article 6(1)(f)).

Who else processes it

Apple, for iCloud, purchases and crash reports. Google, which hosts the support email. GitHub, which hosts this website. When bank connections launch: Plaid, and Cloudflare, which will host Kept’s servers. Some of these process data in the United States, under the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long it is kept

Data on your devices and in your iCloud stays until you delete it. Data on Kept’s servers is kept only while a bank connection is active, and deleted when it ends. Support emails are kept as long as needed to help you.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete personal data the developer holds about you, and object to or restrict its processing, by writing to keptfinances@gmail.com. You can also export everything in Kept yourself, as a spreadsheet, from Settings. If you are in the EU, you may complain to your data protection authority — in Portugal, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD). US residents have the same rights here, wherever they live, and Kept never sells or shares personal information.

Deleting your data

Deleting Kept removes its data from your iPhone. To remove the copy in iCloud, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, and delete Kept’s data there.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses.

Children

Kept is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what Kept collects, you will be told in the app before the change takes effect, and the new version will appear here with a new date.
