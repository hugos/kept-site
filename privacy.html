Privacy policy

Kept has no server and no account. Nothing you enter leaves your devices, except to your own iCloud if you use it.

What Kept collects

Nothing. Kept has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The developer never receives the figures, entries or statements you put into it.

Where your data is stored

On your iPhone and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud database, which belongs to your Apple Account. Apple stores it encrypted, and the developer has no access to it.

Bank statements

When you import a statement, Kept reads the PDF on your iPhone. The file is never uploaded anywhere.

If Kept can't read a statement, you can choose to send its layout to the developer. Before anything is sent, every letter is replaced with “a” and every number with “9”, keeping only column headings and month names, so no names, amounts or account numbers are included. You see the complete text first, and you send it yourself, by email, from your own mail app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple. Kept learns only whether your subscription is active, from Apple, on your device. It never sees your payment details, and the developer never learns who you are.

If you email us

We receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply. Ask, and we'll delete your messages.

Widgets and Siri

Widgets and Siri read the same data, on your iPhone. Kept marks amounts on the Lock Screen as private, so iOS hides them while your iPhone is locked.

Deleting your data

Deleting Kept removes its data from your iPhone. To remove the copy in iCloud, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, and delete Kept's data there.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement for how it handles them.

Children

Kept is a personal finance app meant for adults and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
