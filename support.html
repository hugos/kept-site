Common questions

How do I get a statement from my bank?

In your bank's app or website, open your account, find Statements, choose a month and save it as a PDF. Then, in Kept, tap + and choose Import a statement. An exported PDF works; a photo or a scan of paper doesn't, because Kept needs the text inside the file.

Kept couldn't read my statement. What now?

Kept checks every row against your running balance, and if the numbers don't add up it imports nothing rather than something wrong. On that screen, tap Send this layout: you'll see a version of the statement with every letter and number masked, which is enough to teach Kept your bank's format. Meanwhile, Add this month by hand gets you going.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

In Kept, open Settings and tap Manage subscription. Or, on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Subscriptions are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I've subscribed, but Kept asks me again.

In Kept, open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for Kept, and your data comes across by itself. It can take a few minutes the first time.

My widget says Open Kept.

Open Kept once so it can update the widget. Widgets show your figures with an active subscription or free trial.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app, then remove its iCloud copy: Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Kept. The developer has no copy to delete, because none was ever sent.
